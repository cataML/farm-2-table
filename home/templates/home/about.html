Who we are

Farm Fresh Market is a digital marketplace designed to connect local farmers directly with consumers. We believe in fair trade, fresh food, and empowering rural economies through technology. By removing middlemen, we ensure farmers earn more and consumers receive affordable, quality produce.

🎯 Our Mission

To revolutionize the food supply chain by making local, organic produce accessible to everyone while supporting small-scale farmers through fair pricing and digital empowerment.

👁 Our Vision

To become the most trusted farm-to-table marketplace in Africa, empowering farmers, nourishing communities, and inspiring a shift toward sustainability and transparency in food systems.

🌱 Core Values

  • Transparency: We build trust between farmers and buyers.
  • Sustainability: We promote eco-friendly agricultural practices.
  • Fairness: We ensure farmers are paid fairly and promptly.
  • Community: We support local economies and relationships.

What is Farm-to-Table?

Farm-to-table means your food travels a shorter distance from where it’s grown to your plate. Instead of sitting in warehouses or supermarkets for days (or weeks), your fruits, vegetables, dairy, and meats come fresh from local farms, often harvested just before delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?
Add products to your cart, fill out your shipping info, and complete payment at checkout.
What payment methods are accepted?
We support M-Pesa, PayPal, and bank transfers for your convenience.
Can I cancel or change my order?
Yes, contact our support team within 2 hours to change or cancel your order.
How fresh are the fruits and vegetables?
We guarantee same-day or next-day harvest and delivery. Your produce is picked fresh from the farm and delivered within 24–48 hours to maintain quality and nutrition.
Do you offer subscriptions or weekly baskets?
Yes! You can subscribe to receive weekly or biweekly baskets of seasonal produce. Customize your basket or let us pick the best of the week for you.
Can I become a supplier or farmer partner?
Absolutely! If you're a farmer interested in partnering with us, please visit our Become a Supplier page or contact us directly.
